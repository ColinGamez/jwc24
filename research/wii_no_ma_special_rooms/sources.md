## Contemporary and secondary routing sources

- Inside Games, launch-week hands-on review (2009-05-04). Identifies the four
  initial Company Room sponsors as Seven & i, Unilever, Honda, and AEON;
  connects Seven & i to `自然Wiiテレビ 動物たち`, and describes Unilever's
  LUX origin story and Honda's Insight story:
  https://www.inside-games.jp/article/2009/05/04/35078.html
- Wapa, launch-day first impression (2009-05-01). Independently describes
  the four original rooms' company-introduction/questionnaire format,
  Unilever household-product content including a drain-slime-removal video,
  and Honda's `ある日突然グリーンマシーン` story:
  https://wapa.hatenablog.jp/entry/20090501/p1
- Kizakura, contemporary `たてもの探検隊` post (2009-10-27). Explicitly
  states that its videos and song were viewable through Wii no Ma:
  https://kizakura48000.livedoor.blog/archives/2009-10.html
- 山下漂流, contemporary Wii/DS update log (2009-2011). The 2009-11-21
  entry records the `会社の間` to `いろんな間` rename and the launch cohort
  containing Lotte Ghana and Japan Post; December entries record exact opening
  observations for Meiko Gijuku, Aflac Duck, Glico, and Warner. It also records
  Daiwa House Room disappearing on 2009-11-07. Later entries preserve room
  rotations and feature observations:
  https://neluzik.sakura.ne.jp/
- GameBusiness.jp, Yoshimoto room mention, 2009-09-10:
  https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2009/09/10/361.html
- Inside Games, 2012 Fukubukuro and Mii room campaign, 2012-01-01:
  https://www.inside-games.jp/article/2012/01/01/53721.html
- The contemporary Wii/DS update log above also preserves three unusually
  specific room activities: Nikon's 2010-08-01 return with its photography
  tutorials and two `イン・ザ・パーツ` videos; ROHM's science tour of Chion-in
  and electronic-kit giveaway; and EDION's May 2011 Flipnote Hatena pet-drawing
  contest, including its dates and prizes. These details are used as factual
  gallery cards, not as recovered original media.
- Tacoya3, Valentine/Honda/Nagatanien campaign report with screenshots:
  https://www.tacoya3.com/games/wii/wii_valentine/
- WiiLink Wiki raw page obtained through its MediaWiki API; useful as a lead,
  not sufficient evidence by itself:
  https://wiilink.wiki.gg/api.php?action=parse&page=List_of_original_Wii_Room_content&prop=wikitext&format=json
- Preserved Yoshimoto concierge dialogue identifying Hiroyuki Yabe:
  https://www.reddit.com/r/translator/comments/jvkvnr/japanese_english_couple_of_dialogue_images_from_a/
