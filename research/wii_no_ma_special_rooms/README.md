# Wii no Ma special-room and parade research

This directory tracks historical evidence for the original Wii no Ma
`いろんな間` (Various Rooms) and its parade. It is intentionally separate
from the live JWC24 room configuration: nothing here should be treated as an
authentic reconstruction until its evidence has been checked.

The current roster contains **48 room/campaign records**. This count treats the
three separately documented annual `福袋の間` editions as separate campaign
records. It excludes four trademark-only concepts (`本の間`, `絵本の間`,
`まんがの間`, and `地方の間`) for which no evidence of a public launch has
been found. See `roster_audit.md` for the coverage audit and remaining limits.

## Evidence grades

- **A — primary:** Nintendo/Wii no Ma or participating-company material from
  the period, including archived official pages and press releases.
- **B — contemporary:** dated reporting, blogs, screenshots, or recordings
  made while the original service was active.
- **C — retrospective:** later community documentation or recollection.
- **D — inferred:** a plausible detail derived from client/server behavior,
  without surviving historical proof.

An existence claim and an asset claim are graded separately. For example, an
official release may prove a room's name and dates while providing no usable
copy of its parade Mii.

## What must be recovered for each room

1. Canonical Japanese name, partner, start/end dates, and room type.
2. Parade appearance: Mii name, Mii binary if preserved, clothing/logo, banner,
   entrance image, and parade order.
3. Page structure: menu item count/order and action types (video, picture,
   poll, giveaway, coupon, link, or other client action).
4. Content: titles, descriptions, thumbnails, video/CM identity, polls,
   response screens, giveaway rules, and calendar tie-ins.
5. Visual evidence: full-room footage/screenshots rather than isolated logos.
6. Rights/provenance: preserved original, recreated metadata, replacement
   content, or JWC24-original material.

## Confirmed architectural facts

- The parade presented Miis associated with services and partners; selecting a
  Mii opened that service's room.
- Rooms could contain information, video, polls, giveaways, and partner offers.
- Rooms were not all permanent and included seasonal campaigns.
- The Nintendo interview describes the feature broadening from company rooms
  into `いろんな間`, so later rooms should not be assumed to be corporate.
- The DSi companion could receive video coupons supplied by partner companies.

## Strong first reconstruction target

`永谷園生姜部の間` has unusually complete first-party documentation. The
partner's release establishes its dates (2009-12-18 through 2010-02-28), three
featured recipes, the `生姜部の歌`, a group-activity introduction, a 200-person
ginger-lemon-tea sampling campaign, an application entry point inside the room,
and a related Friday calendar series. The release also contains room and
entrance screenshots that need to be extracted and studied at original size.

## Parade behavior recovered from Nintendo Online Magazine No.146

- `いろんな間` was entered by selecting the houseplant or the lower-right
  movement icon in the main room.
- The user selected a room by pointing at the Mii carrying that room's placard
  in the parade.
- `みんなの間` accepted themed photo submissions, displayed selected entries,
  and let Wii no Ma users vote for a Grand Prix winner.
- The winning creator's Mii became the room host and appeared in the parade
  carrying the `みんなの間` placard. Every featured creator received a stamp
  made from their own Mii.
- `プレゼントの間` used two characterized guide Miis rather than a generic
  logo mascot: a European wife and Japanese husband, an international couple
  in their forties. This proves that parade characters could have narrative
  identities independent of a corporate spokesperson.
- Nintendo called the August 2010 campaign its third present campaign, after
  January's `福袋の間` and late March's `新しいこと はじめよう！の間`.

These details make the placard, host identity, and room behavior first-class
reconstruction data—not decorative extras.

## Research rules

- Never label a recreated celebrity/company Mii as an original dump.
- Preserve source URLs and capture dates beside every extracted fact.
- Do not infer an end date merely from the next known update.
- Keep original room metadata distinct from replacement videos/assets.
- A logo or period commercial proves branding/content availability only when a
  source connects it to Wii no Ma.

## Availability-window caution

`official_directory_snapshots.csv` records only that a room was visible on a
captured date. The first observed capture is not automatically its opening date,
and the last observed capture is not automatically its closing date. It does,
however, give us reliable lower/upper bounds and exposes room rotation over time.
