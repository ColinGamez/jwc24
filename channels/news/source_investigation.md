# Current article-source investigation

No default publisher or live feed has been selected. Restoring the native
format and task path is separate from acquiring actual Japanese articles and
verifying their reuse scope. No article feed was imported into channel data.

Government Public Relations Online has an official
[RSS description](https://www.gov-online.go.jp/rss/). It is a candidate for
public-sector updates, with narrower coverage than a general news publisher.
Its Japanese [content terms](https://www.gov-online.go.jp/tos/) describe PDL1.0
plus site-specific rules, attribution and exceptions; a separate
[content-rules page](https://www.gov-online.go.jp/tos/content_rules/) identifies
additional limits. This does not establish permission for every embedded image,
logo, third-party article or linked ministry resource.

On October 26 the browser search index exposed those official descriptions,
but direct page retrieval returned HTTP403. The exact feed document, live
availability and full item-level reuse scope were not verified. NHK's news
domain was blocked to this browser by robots rules; no access control was
bypassed and no NHK API, article or blanket redistribution permission is assumed.

The owner's preferred feed/API is being requested while native work continues.
Any eventual adapter needs actual source dates, text, attribution and provenance;
missing article, image or geographic metadata cannot be invented. These source
notes do not change original channel UI or select a replacement publisher.
